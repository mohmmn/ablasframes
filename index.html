About me

An independent photographer, I work on film and digital alike, driven by one shared obsession: natural light, the unposed moment, the imperfection that rings true.

Film imposes its own pace: a handful of frames, no screen to check. You wait, you look twice, and the light decides.

Every journey becomes a gathering of fragments — faces, landscapes, silences the eye usually forgets. I bring them back the way one brings back letters from a distant land.

Fragments of a reality that is mine, held out to you — a way of looking at the world, at the height of silence.
