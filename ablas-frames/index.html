About me

I am an independent photographer working in both film and digital, with one shared obsession: natural light, the unposed moment, the imperfection that tells the truth.

Every journey becomes a harvest of fragments — faces, landscapes, silences the eye usually forgets. I bring them home the way one brings back letters from a faraway land.

Fragments of a reality that belongs to me, held out to you — a way of looking at the world, at the height of silence.
